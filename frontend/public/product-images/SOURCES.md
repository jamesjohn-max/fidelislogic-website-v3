# Brand product image standard

Brand product cards must use an original manufacturer-hosted product image or an official manufacturer product-interface screenshot. Save an optimized local WebP copy under `/product-images/<brand>/`, keep the image aligned with the named product family, add specific alt text, and record the official source below. Generic stock photography, unrelated office scenes, repeated placeholders, AI-generated product imagery, and video thumbnails are not acceptable unless specifically approved for that page.
