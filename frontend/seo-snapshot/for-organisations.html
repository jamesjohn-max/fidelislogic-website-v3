Make a Technology Decision You Can Defend

Fidelis Logic assesses what your spaces and teams actually need, compares the credible approaches, and writes the recommendation down — then works alongside your existing reseller or system integrator to get it delivered. We do not sell the hardware, which is what keeps the advice about fit.

Where workplace technology decisions get stuck

  1. Every vendor's proposal looks the same, with no shared basis for comparison.
  2. Nobody can say what the room actually needs, so the specification is a guess.
  3. The decision has to survive scrutiny from IT, facilities, finance and procurement.
  4. There is an existing reseller relationship nobody wants to replace.

How an engagement runs

  1. Understand the business and the space.
  2. Assess requirements and current technology.
  3. Recommend and document an appropriate solution.
  4. Support delivery through your preferred partner.

We work through your reseller, not around them

You keep the commercial relationship, the quotation, the supply and the warranty where they are today. We provide the assessment, the design and the documentation, and stay involved through delivery so the solution that was agreed is the solution that gets installed.

Because we are not paid on the hardware, a more expensive room is not a better outcome for us.
