The technology side of your move, handled

An office move can undo years of workspace investment in a weekend. We plan and execute the workspace-technology side of any relocation: cataloguing what moves and what's replaced, coordinating with movers and fit-out teams, and recommissioning every room in the new space so day-one meetings just work.

Common challenges

Technology is an afterthought. Furniture and fit-out get detailed plans while displays, codecs and panels are boxed up at the last minute.

Day-one meetings fail. Rooms go live with missing cables, wrong resource accounts or displays mounted in the wrong place.

Moving kit that should be retired. Old devices are carefully transported to the new office, only to be replaced a few months later.

How the engagement runs

  1. Plan: A move-out audit and inventory decides what moves, what is retired and what the new space needs.
  2. Decommission: Devices are safely removed, labelled, packed and tracked, with optional storage between sites.
  3. Reinstall: Kit is installed at the destination, coordinated with your fit-out contractor, movers and IT team.
  4. Recommission: Every room is recommissioned, calendar-synced and test-called before your team's first day.

Frequently asked questions

When should we involve you in an office move?

As early as possible, ideally while the new floor plan is being designed. That leaves time to plan data points, power, display positions and acoustics for each room instead of retrofitting them.

Do you coordinate with our movers and fit-out contractor?

Yes. We work to one project plan with your movers, fit-out contractor and IT team, so technology work is scheduled around the build and the move date.

Is our equipment insured during the move?

Yes. Devices are inventoried and tracked door to door, with insured transport and secure staging.

Can you store equipment between move-out and move-in?

Yes. Optional storage is available when there is a gap between leaving the old office and the new space being ready.

Should we move old equipment or replace it?

It depends on age, condition and support status. We flag devices worth leaving behind or trading in, so you are not paying to move kit that will be replaced soon after.
