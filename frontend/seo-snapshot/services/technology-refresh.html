Meeting room & AV technology refresh in the UAE

Refresh without the disruption. Plan, phase, and swap. Structured refresh programmes for meeting rooms, headsets and workspace platforms. End-of-life devices retired, replacements phased in, users trained on what changes.

New technology in, old estate out, no lost meetings

Devices age. Vendors change ownership. Platforms sunset features. When a refresh is due, the question isn't just what to buy. It's how to swap without breaking the workday. We design phased migrations that pilot, prove, and roll out the new estate while retiring the old, with change comms and user training baked into the plan.

Common challenges

Devices reaching end of support. Codecs, cameras and panels are losing vendor support, and security updates are about to stop.

Call quality keeps slipping. Ageing headsets and room audio make hybrid meetings harder to follow, and complaints keep rising.

Worry about disruption. Swapping dozens of rooms at once risks leaving teams without a working space in critical weeks.

How the engagement runs

  1. Assess: Confirm what is end-of-life, compare total cost of ownership and document feature parity.
  2. Pilot: New kit is proven in pilot rooms or departments against agreed success criteria.
  3. Swap: Replacements are staged and swapped by room, floor or site, so users always have a working room.
  4. Retire: Old equipment is decommissioned and either recycled through certified e-waste channels or traded in.

What's included

  3. Procurement and staging of replacement kit
  4. Coordinated swap-outs by room, floor, or site
  5. Certified e-waste disposal or trade-in of the old estate

Frequently asked questions

How do we know when it's time to refresh meeting room technology?

Common signs are devices reaching end of vendor support, rising incident rates, falling call quality and platforms dropping features. A workspace audit gives you a replace-or-retain call for every asset.

Will users lose access to rooms during the refresh?

No. Pilot rooms are proven first, and swaps are phased by room, floor or site, so there is always a working room available.

What happens to our old equipment?

It is decommissioned and either recycled through a certified e-waste partner or traded in for credit where possible.

Can we change platforms as part of a refresh?

Yes. Many refreshes include a platform change, such as consolidating onto Microsoft Teams Rooms after a merger. Feature parity, user training and change communications are planned into the programme.

Do you refresh headsets as well as meeting rooms?

Yes. Headset fleets more than five years old are a common refresh, often combined with persona-based standardisation.
