How the engagement runs

  1. Scope: Share the room size, platform and date, and we match the right video bar or modular system and accessories.
  2. Prepare: Kit is configured, updated, signed in and test-called in our workshop before it leaves.
  3. Deliver: We deliver, install and verify a joining call on site, with an optional technician for the session.
  4. Collect: After the event the kit is collected, session data is wiped, and you receive a short usage report.

Frequently asked questions

What video conferencing equipment can I rent?

Enterprise video bars and modular room systems such as Poly Studio, Neat Bar, Logitech Rally and Jabra PanaCast, plus headsets, remote controls and cabling. Kit is matched to your platform and room size.

Which platforms does the rental kit support?

Rental systems are configured for Microsoft Teams, Zoom or Google Meet, or supplied as BYOD kit that works with any laptop and meeting platform.

Is installation included in the rental?

Yes. Delivery, installation, a joining-call test and collection are included. An on-site AV technician can be added for high-stakes sessions such as board meetings or keynote calls.

What happens to our data after the rental?

Every system is signed out and wiped of accounts, call history and session data when it is collected.

Can we rent a system as a pilot before buying?

Yes. Renting a pilot room is a practical way to test a platform and room layout with real users before committing to a full rollout.
