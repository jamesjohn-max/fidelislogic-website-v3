Managed AV & meeting room support in the UAE

Keep every room, every device, every meeting running. SLA-backed managed services for meeting rooms, workspace platforms and collaboration devices: proactive monitoring, remote remediation and lifecycle firmware management.

Problems fixed before users notice them

Post go-live is where most of the value is realised, or lost. Our managed service keeps meeting rooms, video devices, and workspace platforms healthy through continuous monitoring, firmware baselining, incident response, and quarterly service reviews. We plug directly into your ITSM tool so tickets never sit in a separate queue.

Common challenges

Users report problems before IT knows. A room drops offline or loses calendar sync, and the first alert is a frustrated email from a meeting organiser.

Firmware drifts room by room. Without a baseline, devices run different versions, and a fix that works in one room fails in the next.

Tickets fall between teams. AV issues bounce between IT, facilities and the vendor, and nobody owns the resolution.

How the engagement runs

  1. Onboard: Every room and device is baselined, severity levels are agreed, and monitoring connects to your ITSM tool.
  2. Monitor: Devices and platforms are watched around the clock for offline states, peripheral faults and sync failures.
  3. Resolve: Incidents are fixed remotely where possible and escalated to on-site support where needed, within agreed SLAs.
  4. Review: Quarterly service reviews cover incidents, utilisation trends and recommended improvements.

Frequently asked questions

What does managed meeting room support include?

24/7 monitoring of rooms, devices and platforms, SLA-backed incident response, remote remediation, firmware management, ITSM integration and quarterly service reviews. On-site support can be added for regulated environments.

Can you support meeting rooms you didn't install?

Yes. Rooms installed by others are baselined during onboarding, so we know their condition and configuration before support starts.

Will our team need to use a new ticketing portal?

No. Tickets flow through your existing tool, such as ServiceNow, Jira or Freshservice, so your team keeps a single queue.

How is managed support priced?

As a monthly subscription per room, device fleet or platform, covering monitoring, remediation, firmware and quarterly reviews in a single line item.

Do you support estates across several emirates?

Yes, including multi-site estates across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, the Northern Emirates and the wider GCC.
