A clear brief before anyone quotes a price

We work alongside your business, IT and facilities leaders to translate real workplace outcomes into a clear technology brief. That means aligning stakeholders, benchmarking options across vendors, sizing the commercial envelope, and producing a recommendation you can defend to your board, before a single purchase order is raised.

Common challenges

Every proposal says its platform is the answer. Proposals arrive shaped around what each supplier sells, which makes them hard to compare like for like.

Stakeholders want different things. IT wants manageability, facilities wants utilisation data, and leadership wants a number it can approve.

No business case to take to the board. Without costed options and success measures, projects stall at budget review or get approved on gut feel.

How the engagement runs

  1. Discover: Workshops with business, IT and facilities teams to capture how people actually meet, work and book space.
  2. Assess: A review of current rooms, devices, licences and network readiness, separating what works from what doesn't.
  3. Compare: Fit-for-purpose options scored across vendors against your requirements, budget and support model.
  4. Recommend: A costed, phased recommendation and executive summary, ready for board approval.

Frequently asked questions

What does a workplace technology consultant do?

A workplace technology consultant helps you decide what to buy, and why, before you commit budget. We translate business goals into a technology brief, compare options across vendors, and give you a costed recommendation for meeting rooms, headsets and workspace platforms.

How long does a consulting engagement take?

Most engagements move from kickoff to a signed-off recommendation in 4 to 8 weeks. Larger multi-site programmes can continue as retained advisory alongside the rollout.

Can you work with our existing reseller or system integrator?

Yes. Our recommendation and specification can be delivered through your preferred reseller or system integrator, so you keep existing supplier relationships while gaining an independent view.

Which platforms do you advise on?

Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms, Google Meet hardware and BYOD meeting spaces, plus enterprise headsets and room booking and workspace platforms. We compare hardware from brands including Poly, Neat, Logitech, Yealink and Jabra.

Where in the UAE do you offer consulting?

We work on-site with organisations across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the Northern Emirates.
