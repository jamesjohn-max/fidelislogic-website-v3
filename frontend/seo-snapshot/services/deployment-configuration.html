Once a design is signed off, execution is where most projects wobble. We take ownership from procurement through commissioning: site surveys, structured cabling coordination, device provisioning, calendar integration, network readiness, acoustic tuning, and end-user validation. Every room ships with a one-page quick-start guide and a joining-test video call recorded during handover.

Common challenges

Too many parties, no single owner. The AV installer, network team, calendar admins and fit-out contractor each own a piece, and gaps appear between them.

Every room behaves differently. Without a common standard, settings vary room to room and users never know what to expect.

Surprises on day one. Calendar sync, firewall rules or camera framing issues surface in front of the people the room was built for.

How the engagement runs

  1. Survey: A site survey per room covering power, data points, display positions, acoustics and network readiness.
  2. Stage: Devices are provisioned, updated and configured to your platform standard before they reach site.
  3. Install: Installation, cabling coordination, calendar integration, acoustic tuning and camera framing, room by room.
  4. Accept: Each room passes the same acceptance test, including a live joining call, before sign-off and handover.

Frequently asked questions

Which meeting room platforms do you deploy?

We deploy Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms, Google Meet hardware and BYOD rooms using hardware from brands including Poly, Neat, Logitech, Yealink and Jabra, along with room booking panels and workspace platforms.

Can you deploy meeting rooms across multiple sites?

Yes. Multi-room rollouts from 5 to more than 100 spaces are planned as a pilot room first, then deployed in waves by floor or site.

Do you handle Microsoft 365 and calendar integration?

Yes. Room resource accounts, calendar sync with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and device sign-in are configured and tested as part of every deployment.

What happens after go-live?

Every room comes with a quick-start guide, as-built documentation and 30 days of hypercare. Monitoring and support can then continue under our managed support service.

Can you work alongside our fit-out contractor?

Yes. We coordinate cabling, containment and display mounting with your fit-out contractor and IT team, so the technology work runs on the same programme as the build.
