Workplace technology training & user adoption in the UAE

Technology only pays back when people actually use it. Role-based training and change management that lifts adoption of meeting rooms, workspace platforms and collaboration tools, measured by real usage.

Adoption measured in usage, not attendance

The best-designed meeting room is worthless if users default to Zooming from their laptop. We build role-based training and adoption programmes tailored to how your teams actually work: short walkthrough videos, floor-walker support during the first two weeks, executive champion sessions and adoption dashboards.

Common challenges

People default to their laptop. Users skip the room system and join from their own device, so the investment sits idle.

One-size-fits-all training. Executives, assistants and IT champions sit through the same generic session, and most of it doesn't apply.

Know-how leaves with the installer. After handover nobody internal can answer quick questions or onboard new starters.

How the engagement runs

  1. Map: Personas and daily journeys are mapped to see where people struggle with rooms, booking and collaboration tools.
  2. Build: Role-based content: short video walkthroughs per room type, cheat sheets and one-pagers.
  3. Launch: Live sessions and floor-walker support during go-live weeks, plus executive and champion coaching.
  4. Measure: Adoption dashboards and 30, 60 and 90-day reviews track real usage, not attendance.

Frequently asked questions

What does a user adoption programme include?

Persona mapping, a role-based curriculum, short video walkthroughs, quick-reference guides, floor-walker support during go-live, and adoption reviews at 30, 60 and 90 days.

How do you measure user adoption?

We compare room utilisation and platform engagement data before and after training, rather than counting attendance.

Can you train users on Microsoft Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms?

Yes. Content is built per room type and platform, covering how to join, share content, use the touch controller and get help.

Do you support platform migrations?

Yes. Moves such as Zoom to Microsoft Teams, or Google to Microsoft, need change communications and training alongside the technical cut-over, and we plan both together.

Is training delivered in person?

Live sessions and floor-walker support are delivered on site at your offices across the UAE, backed by short videos and guides people can use any time.
