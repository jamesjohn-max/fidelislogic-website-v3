Room Planner

Turn the customer's ask into a documented room plan, keeping sales, presales, implementation and the customer aligned before install — so no one hears "This isn't what I asked for!"

What Room Planner does

  1. Capture the room's current design and the customer's requirements in one place
  2. Close the gap between what the customer needs and what gets installed
  3. Cut down on repeat site visits
  4. Produce a document ready to share with the customer and your technical team or distributor, so they can prepare a BOQ quickly
